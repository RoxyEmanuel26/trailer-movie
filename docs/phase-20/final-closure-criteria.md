# Final Closure Criteria

## Overview
Master Sign-Off approves the launch, but the project phase itself is only officially "closed" when the transition to the maintenance team is complete.

## Conditions for Closure
The project lifecycle is considered closed when all the following conditions are met:

1.  **No Unresolved Blockers:** The Final Quality Gate was passed.
2.  **All Sign-Offs Collected:** The Master Sign-Off is formally recorded.
3.  **Documentation Complete:** The final documentation review passed, and documents are stored in the designated central repository.
4.  **Deviations Recorded:** All architectural drift is documented in the Phase Decision Logs.
5.  **Risks Accepted:** The Risk Acceptance Model has been signed by the Product Owner.
6.  **Handoff Complete:** The operations and maintenance team has successfully taken control of the production environment, including credentials and monitoring alerts.
7.  **Backlog Transferred:** All open, non-blocking issues have been moved to the maintenance backlog and assigned to the maintenance owner.

## Formal Closure
Once these criteria are met, the project development team is officially decoupled from daily operations. Any future work requires a new, prioritized ticket in the maintenance backlog or the initiation of a new formal project phase.
