# Phase 20 Summary

## Final Audit & Master Sign-Off

Phase 20 defines the strict framework for evaluating whether the project is truly complete and ready for public launch and operational handoff. It shifts the project from "building" to "verifying and closing."

### Core Strategies Established:
*   **Audit Scope & Source of Truth:** Defined exactly what must be audited (from SEO to Security) and established that the implemented system must be verified against the approved Phase 2 architecture.
*   **Completeness & Gaps:** Created objective criteria for what constitutes a "complete" feature, and defined a framework for identifying and categorizing gaps as Blockers, Tech Debt, or Descoped items.
*   **Risk Acceptance:** Acknowledged that zero risk is impossible, establishing a formal model for the Product Owner to explicitly accept residual risks.
*   **Final Quality Gate:** Established a mandatory checklist of automated and manual tests that must pass before sign-off can occur.
*   **Master Sign-Off Model:** Defined a delegated approval model where subject matter experts (Security, QA, SEO) sign off on their domains before the Product Owner gives the final Master Sign-Off.
*   **Closure & Stewardship:** Outlined the criteria for officially closing the project phase and handing over ownership to the maintenance team, ensuring the product is ready for long-term survival.

### Key Outcomes:
The project now has a defensive, explicit closing mechanism. This prevents the project from lingering in a state of "almost done" and ensures that when Master Sign-Off is given, all stakeholders share a documented understanding of the system's exact state, risks, and operational readiness.
