# Master Sign-Off Model

## Overview
The Master Sign-Off is the formal acknowledgment that the project has met its requirements and is ready for public release and operational transition. It relies on a delegated model where subject matter experts sign off on their respective areas before final executive approval.

## Area Sign-Offs
Before the Master Sign-Off can occur, the following domain-specific sign-offs must be collected:

1.  **Technical Sign-off:** (Lead Engineer) Confirms the architecture, code quality, and deployment pipeline meet standards.
2.  **Security Sign-off:** (Security Lead / Lead Engineer) Confirms the risk acceptance model is complete and no unacceptable vulnerabilities exist.
3.  **SEO Sign-off:** (Marketing/SEO Lead) Confirms indexing and metadata strategies are correctly implemented.
4.  **Performance Sign-off:** (Lead Engineer) Confirms Core Web Vitals and load testing targets are met.
5.  **QA Sign-off:** (QA Lead) Confirms the test plan is fully executed and no blocking bugs remain.
6.  **Operations Sign-off:** (DevOps/Ops Lead) Confirms monitoring, backups, and runbooks are ready.
7.  **Content Sign-off:** (Content Lead) Confirms the initial dataset is accurate and the admin CMS is usable.

## Master Sign-Off
*   **Owner:** Product Owner (or Client/Executive Sponsor).
*   **Meaning:** By providing Master Sign-Off, the Product Owner agrees that:
    *   All Area Sign-Offs have been collected.
    *   The delivered product meets the business goals outlined in Phase 1.
    *   All residual risks logged in the Risk Acceptance Model are understood and accepted by the business.
*   **Mechanism:** An explicit, written confirmation (e.g., an email, a signed document, or an approved Jira Epic) referencing the Final Quality Gate artifacts.
