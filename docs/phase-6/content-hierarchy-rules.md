# Content Hierarchy and Presentation Rules

Visual hierarchy guides the user's eye. On a trailer site, the hierarchy is strictly: Video > Artwork > Typography.

## 1. Heading Hierarchy
- **H1:** Reserved exclusively for the primary subject of the page (e.g., The Movie Title on a detail page, or "Trending Trailers" on a list page).
- **H2:** Used for major section breaks (e.g., "Cast & Crew", "More Like This").
- **H3:** Used for minor labels within sections.

## 2. Card Layout Behavior (Posters)
- The fundamental unit of browsing is the "Movie Poster Card".
- **Aspect Ratio:** Fixed 2:3 aspect ratio.
- **Hover State:** When a user hovers over a poster, a subtle transform (scale 1.05) occurs, and a translucent "Play" button icon fades in over the center, clearly indicating the primary action.

## 3. Synopsis Density
- Long blocks of text break the visual aesthetic.
- **Rule:** Synopses on the movie detail page are truncated at 3 lines by default. A small "Read More" button expands the text block if the user is interested, preserving the page layout.
