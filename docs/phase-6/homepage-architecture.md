# Homepage Architecture

The homepage is a curated showcase designed to pull users immediately into the content.

## Section Order

### 1. Hero Section (Above the Fold)
- **Content:** A massive, edge-to-edge backdrop image or silent auto-playing background video of the #1 featured movie.
- **UI:** Movie logo (or bold typography), a short punchy tagline, and a prominent "Watch Trailer" button.
- **Why:** Delivers immediate cinematic impact.

### 2. Trending Now (Carousel)
- **Content:** Horizontal scrolling list of the top 10 currently popular movies.
- **Why:** Capitalizes on current pop culture zeitgeist; highly clickable.

### 3. Upcoming Releases (Carousel)
- **Content:** Movies hitting theaters or streaming in the next 30-90 days.
- **Why:** Users often visit trailer sites to see "what's coming out next."

### 4. Curated Collections / Genres (Stacked Rows)
- **Content:** E.g., "Epic Sci-Fi", "Netflix Originals", "Horror Hits". Controlled by the Admin Homepage Builder.
- **Why:** Provides guided discovery for users who don't know exactly what they are looking for.

### 5. Monetization-Safe Placement
- **Content:** A horizontal ad slot inserted between the 3rd and 4th rows.
- **Why:** Placed far enough down so as not to ruin the initial visual impression, but high enough to guarantee impressions.

### 6. Recently Added (Grid)
- **Content:** A chronological grid of the newest trailers added to the database.
- **Why:** Good for SEO (keeps the homepage content fresh) and for daily returning users.
