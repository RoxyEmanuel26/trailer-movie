# Static Content Pages

While not dynamic, static pages are essential for establishing trust with users and adhering to legal requirements, which directly impacts search engine trust scores (E-E-A-T).

## 1. Required Pages
- **`/about`:** Explains the purpose of the site (curated movie trailers).
- **`/contact`:** A simple form or email address for business inquiries.
- **`/privacy` & `/terms`:** Standard legal boilerplate, essential for approval by ad networks (like Google AdSense).
- **`/dmca`:** Critical for a media site. Explains that videos are embedded from YouTube and provides instructions for copyright takedown requests to protect the platform from liability.

## 2. Architecture
- Rendered statically at build time.
- Use a simple, narrow-column text layout for readability. No complex grids or sidebars required.
- Linked exclusively in the global footer to keep the primary header navigation clean and focused on content discovery.
