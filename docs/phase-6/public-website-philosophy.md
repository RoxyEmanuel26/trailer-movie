# Public Website Philosophy

## Purpose
The public website is a high-performance discovery engine for movie trailers. Its primary goal is to get users to click "Play" on a trailer as quickly as possible, while providing enough surrounding context (synopsis, cast, release date) to satisfy their curiosity.

## What the site should feel like
- **Cinematic:** Dark mode exclusive. Large, high-quality artwork (posters and backdrops) should dominate the viewport.
- **Fast:** Pages must load instantly. Videos should not block the initial page render.
- **Frictionless:** No pop-ups blocking the content, no auto-playing videos with sound without user intent, and no labyrinthine navigation menus.

## What it should avoid
- **Information Overload:** We are not Wikipedia or IMDb. We do not need a 5,000-word biography of the 3rd grip on a movie set. The focus is on the *Trailer* and the core metadata.
- **Intrusive Ads:** Monetization is important, but above-the-fold content must prioritize the movie artwork and the play button.

## The Balance (Entertainment vs. SEO)
- **Entertainment:** Users come for the visual thrill of a trailer. The UI must be highly visual.
- **SEO:** Search engines cannot watch videos. Therefore, the visual UI must be underpinned by rich text (synopses, cast lists, structured data) hidden semantically or pushed slightly below the fold to ensure the page ranks highly for "{Movie} Trailer" queries.
