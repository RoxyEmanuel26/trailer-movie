# Monetization Placement Guidelines

## Safe Ad Placement Rules
The goal is to allow programmatic display ads without destroying the cinematic feel or incurring SEO penalties (like the Google Page Layout algorithm penalty).

1. **Top Banner (Above the Fold):**
   - **Rule:** A single, standard leaderboard ad (728x90) is permitted at the very top of the page, above the navigation bar.
   - **Constraint:** It must not push the primary content (the video player or hero image) below the fold on desktop.

2. **Sidebar Ad (Below the Fold):**
   - **Rule:** A sticky sidebar ad (300x250 or 300x600) is permitted alongside secondary content (like the synopsis or cast list).
   - **Constraint:** It must never overlap the video player or poster image.

3. **In-Feed Native Ads:**
   - **Rule:** When displaying grids of movie posters (e.g., 4 columns), an ad block can replace one of the poster slots.
   - **Constraint:** The ad block must perfectly match the aspect ratio and grid gap of the posters around it to maintain visual flow.

## Forbidden Placements (Strict Rules)
To preserve the premium feel, the following are strictly prohibited:
- **No Pop-ups or Interstitials:** Absolutely no ads that overlay the screen and require the user to hunt for an "X" to close them.
- **No Auto-playing Audio/Video Ads:** Outstream video ads competing with the actual trailer audio are forbidden.
- **No Ads Inside the Player Container:** The theater section (the black background containing the video facade) is sacred. No banner ads may touch this area.

## Affiliate Link Placement
- **Where:** "Buy Tickets" or "Stream Now" buttons should be placed immediately below the primary movie metadata (Release date/Runtime), next to the primary "Play Trailer" CTA.
- **Styling:** Affiliate buttons should use the Secondary Button style (Outline or Surface Level 2) so they don't visually compete with the Primary Red trailer button.
