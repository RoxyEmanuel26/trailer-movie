# Competitor Analysis

## Analyzed Competitors
1. **IMDb (Internet Movie Database)**
2. **TrailerAddict**
3. **Rotten Tomatoes**
4. **YouTube (Movies / Trailers Channels)**

---

## Common Patterns Identified

### Homepage Patterns
- **Hero Carousel:** The top of the page is almost universally dedicated to the biggest upcoming releases, featuring full-width backdrops or auto-playing silent video clips.
- **Grid Layouts:** Below the fold, competitors use structured grids (e.g., "In Theaters", "Coming Soon", "Trending") utilizing vertical movie posters.
- **Dark Mode Default:** Most trailer sites use a dark color scheme (blacks and dark grays) to mimic a theater environment and make vibrant movie posters stand out.

### Movie Detail Page Patterns
- **Player Prominence:** The video player is the focal point, usually spanning the full width of the content container at the top of the page.
- **Metadata Hierarchy:** Immediately below the player is the title, release year, runtime, and MPAA rating, followed by the synopsis and cast.
- **Sidebar Posters:** A high-resolution poster is commonly displayed to the left or right of the primary metadata for visual anchoring.

### Search Behavior Patterns
- **Autocomplete with Images:** When users start typing, dropdown results instantly show the movie title alongside a small thumbnail of the poster.

### Genre Navigation Patterns
- **Dedicated Landing Pages:** Clicking a genre acts as a filter, bringing up a dedicated page (e.g., `/genre/sci-fi`) optimized for that specific category.

---

## SEO Patterns Commonly Used
- **Schema Markup:** Heavy reliance on `VideoObject` and `Movie` structured data to appear as rich snippets (or video carousels) in Google search results.
- **Keyword-Rich URLs:** Formats like `/title/tt1234567/` (IMDb) or `/movie/movie-name-year`.
- **Title Tags:** "Watch [Movie Title] Trailer - Release Date, Cast | [Site Name]"

---

## Monetization Patterns Commonly Used
- **Display Advertising:** Banner ads at the top, sidebar units, and in-feed ads. (Highly prevalent on TrailerAddict and Rotten Tomatoes).
- **Pre-Roll Video Ads:** Ads playing before the trailer (Common on proprietary players; less controllable with YouTube embeds).
- **Affiliate Links:** "Buy Tickets" or "Stream Now" links.

---

## Weaknesses & Gaps in Competitors
- **Visual Clutter:** Sites like IMDb are overwhelmed with ads, trivia, and unrelated content, making it hard to just watch a trailer.
- **Poor Performance:** TrailerAddict and similar niche sites often suffer from slow load times due to heavy ads and unoptimized scripts.
- **Inconsistent Quality:** YouTube searches often yield fan-made trailers or reaction videos instead of the official studio release.

## Our Opportunities / Differentiators
- **Hyper-Focused UX:** Provide a distraction-free, cinematic interface that prioritizes the trailer over trivial metadata.
- **Speed:** Build a highly optimized, fast-loading architecture (unlike legacy competitors).
- **Curated Quality:** Guarantee that every video on the platform is the official, highest-quality version available, avoiding the "clickbait" trap of YouTube.
