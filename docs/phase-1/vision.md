# Vision

## Product Vision
To be the fastest, most engaging platform for discovering upcoming movies through high-quality trailers, with zero clutter and zero distractions.

## Mission
To provide movie enthusiasts and casual viewers with an authentic, streamlined experience to find what to watch next. We aim to respect the user's time by focusing purely on high-quality video content and essential metadata, removing the noise commonly found on massive movie portals.

## Target Audience
- **Movie Enthusiasts & Cinephiles:** Users who actively track upcoming releases and want to see the latest trailers as soon as they drop.
- **Casual Viewers:** People looking for weekend entertainment who want a quick way to browse new and trending movies.
- **Mobile Users:** Users who browse on the go and require a fast, responsive, and mobile-friendly trailer viewing experience.

## What Problems Do We Solve?
- **Cluttered Interfaces:** Sites like IMDb are filled with ads, extensive trivia, and gossip that bury the actual trailer.
- **Clickbait & Unofficial Content:** Searching YouTube often yields fan-made concepts or fake trailers. We provide a curated source of *only* official trailers.
- **Decision Paralysis:** By organizing trailers by genre, trending status, and release date in a visually appealing way, we make it easier to decide what to watch.

## Product Boundaries

### What We Are Building
- A curated platform for official movie trailers and teasers.
- A fast, visually rich browsing experience emphasizing video and poster art.
- A categorized directory (by genre, release date, and trending status).

### What We Are NOT Building
- A platform for full movie streaming or pirated content.
- A user-generated review site or forum (no comments or ratings in Phase 1).
- A celebrity gossip or entertainment news blog.
- A self-hosted video platform (we will rely on high-quality embeds to maintain performance and reduce costs).
