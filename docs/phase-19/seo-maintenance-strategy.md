# SEO Maintenance Strategy

## Overview
Search engine traffic is a primary acquisition channel. SEO is not a one-time launch checklist; it requires ongoing monitoring and adjustment to maintain and grow rankings.

## Index Coverage Review
*   **Cadence:** Weekly.
*   **Action:** Monitor Google Search Console for new "Errors" (e.g., Server errors (5xx), Not found (404)) or "Valid with warnings". Address technical errors immediately.
*   **Unindexed Pages:** Investigate why valid pages are marked "Discovered - currently not indexed" or "Crawled - currently not indexed" to ensure crawl budget isn't being wasted.

## Sitemap Update Review
*   **Cadence:** Monthly.
*   **Action:** Verify that dynamic sitemaps are updating correctly as new movies are added. Ensure no dead links or redirected URLs are included in the sitemap.

## Canonical Integrity Review
*   **Cadence:** Quarterly.
*   **Action:** Check that dynamically generated pages (e.g., search filters, pagination) have appropriate canonical tags pointing to the primary version of the content to prevent duplicate content penalties.

## Title and Description Tuning
*   **Cadence:** Ongoing.
*   **Action:** Review pages ranking on pages 2-3 of SERPs or pages with high impressions but low CTR. Experiment with tweaking `<title>` tags and meta descriptions to improve click-through rates.

## Internal Linking Adjustments
*   **Cadence:** Monthly.
*   **Action:** As new, highly important movies are added, ensure they are linked from high-authority internal pages (like the homepage or popular category pages) to pass PageRank and aid discovery.

## Thin Content Cleanup
*   **Cadence:** Bi-annually.
*   **Action:** Identify pages with very little content (e.g., a movie page with no poster, no description, and no trailer). Either enrich this content, set to `noindex`, or redirect it to a broader category to preserve overall site quality scores.

## New Opportunity Identification
*   **Cadence:** Quarterly.
*   **Action:** Use keyword research tools to identify new search trends (e.g., a sudden spike in a specific actor or a new sub-genre) and create landing pages or categories to capture that traffic.

## Search Performance Review
*   **Cadence:** Monthly.
*   **Action:** Review top-performing keywords and landing pages. Identify drops in rank for core terms and investigate potential causes (competitor changes, algorithm updates, or technical regressions).
