# Phase 19 Summary

## Post-Launch Operations & Continuous Improvement

Phase 19 defines the architecture for maintaining, monitoring, and improving the product safely after launch. It shifts the focus from rapid feature development to stability, data-driven iteration, and proactive maintenance.

### Core Strategies Established:
*   **Philosophy:** Stability first, followed by safe, incremental, and data-driven continuous improvement.
*   **Cadence:** Defined daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly checks to ensure health across all systems.
*   **Incident Triage:** Established clear SEV levels and response expectations to handle production issues effectively.
*   **Maintenance:** Created specific strategies for keeping Content, SEO, and Performance healthy over time, preventing natural degradation.
*   **Governance:** Defined how changes are approved post-launch, separating high-risk architecture changes from routine content updates.
*   **Feedback & Backlog:** Created structured loops to capture user feedback, categorize it, and prioritize it alongside technical debt and new features.

### Key Outcomes:
The product is now equipped with a documented operating model. The team has clear guidelines on how to handle emergencies, how to prioritize competing demands (bugs vs. features vs. tech debt), and how to ensure the product remains healthy long-term while preparing for future evolution.
