# Long-Term Maintenance Health

## Overview
How do we know if the post-launch operations strategy is working? A healthy product exhibits specific long-term characteristics that indicate sustainability and quality.

## Signs of a Healthy Operation

### 1. Stable Uptime
*   The site consistently hits its SLA (e.g., 99.9% uptime). Planned maintenance is communicated in advance and executed without extending into unplanned downtime.

### 2. Stable Search Visibility
*   Organic traffic is not erratic. While algorithm updates may cause minor fluctuations, the overall trend is steady or growing. There are no sudden, unexplained drops to zero for core keywords.

### 3. Stable Performance
*   Core Web Vitals remain consistently green over months, even as the database grows and new media assets are added. Performance is monitored continuously, not just before major releases.

### 4. Regular Content Refresh
*   The homepage never looks abandoned. New trailers appear within hours of official release. Broken links are rare and quickly fixed.

### 5. Low Incident Frequency
*   SEV-1 (Critical) incidents are extremely rare (e.g., less than once a quarter). Most issues caught are SEV-3 or SEV-4 and are caught before users report them.

### 6. Controlled Backlog Growth
*   The backlog is a living priority list, not a graveyard of ignored ideas. Stale tickets are regularly purged, and the total number of open bugs does not continuously climb sprint over sprint.

### 7. Documented Change History
*   Every change in production can be traced back to a PR, an approved decision, and a specific business or technical rationale. If someone asks "Why was this changed six months ago?", the answer is readily available in the documentation or commit history.
