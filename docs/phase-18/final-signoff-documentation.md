# Final Sign-Off Documentation

## The Paper Trail

The final sign-off is the formal transfer of liability and acceptance of the product's state at launch.

### What Must Be Recorded
The `phase-18-decision-log.md` (or a dedicated release ticket) must serve as the final record containing:

1. **Checklist Completion:** A link to the completed Final QA Review Checklist, proving due diligence was performed.
2. **Risk Acceptance Notes:** Explicit statements from the Product Lead accepting any known bugs or deferred features present in the launch build.
3. **Open Issues List:** A link to the issue tracker showing the exact state of the backlog at the time of launch.
4. **Handoff Status:** Confirmation that ownership of the production infrastructure (AWS/Vercel/Database) has been transferred to the client or permanent maintenance team.

### The Approvers
The document must list the names of the individuals who formed the Go/No-Go committee (Product Lead, Tech Lead, etc.) and note their unanimous approval for the launch.

### Purpose
This documentation protects the engineering team from post-launch accusations regarding known bugs, and provides the maintenance team with a clear snapshot of the system's exact state on Day 1.
