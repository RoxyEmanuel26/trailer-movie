# Phase 20 — Final Audit & Master Sign-Off

## Goal
You are working on **Phase 20 only** for a website trailer movie project.

Your task is to define the complete final audit and master sign-off architecture that closes the project lifecycle.

Do **not** create implementation code, audit scripts, deployment scripts, or operational automation logic yet.

The purpose of this phase is to provide the final project-level review framework that confirms the system is complete, consistent, safe, maintainable, and ready for long-term ownership.

---

## Primary Objective
Create a complete final audit and master sign-off plan for a modern movie trailer website that supports:

- Full project audit
- Architecture consistency verification
- Requirement completeness verification
- Implementation completeness verification
- Risk acceptance review
- Final ownership handoff
- Final release sign-off
- Post-project responsibility clarity
- Open issue closure or transfer
- Long-term stewardship readiness

The final audit must be strict, explicit, and defensible.

---

- What must be verified before master sign-off?
- What must never be ignored at the end?

- Why is a formal audit necessary?
